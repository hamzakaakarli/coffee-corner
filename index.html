About Me

I'm a long-time coffee shop enthusiast who's spent years exploring cafés across the U.S. and abroad. I treat cafés as a true third space — part study hall, part social hub — where I learn, read, and meet friends. I'm drawn to vibrant atmospheres that spark conversation and help me stay productive, and I review each spot with both comfort and craft in mind.

Ranking Criteria

I try to go to each place that I review a couple times, at least more than once if possible. I want to create a trend in mmy head of what is going on, and how are they canging and evolving over timme? Is it for the better or the worse? I take into account the first time I visit the place, how it makes me feel. Then I

  • out of 10 Coffee Taste Does it sing? Scored on complexity, balance, temperature, and that all-important first sip. A 10 means you'd cross town for it.
  • out of 10 Value Is the price fair for what lands on the table? Drinks, food, and the feeling of walking away either satisfied or shortchanged all factor in.
  • out of 10 Seat & Stay Are the chairs an afterthought or something that was well thought out? I am the creator of the chair to table ratio (Chair:Table), a proprietary metric that helps characterize how well a seat allows a person to use a table for academmic endeavours (laptop wwrk or reading a physical book). Scored on comfort, table space, noise level, and how long you genuinely want to stay to get work done.
  • out of 10 Cleanliness Counters, bathrooms, floors, cups. Grime is a dealbreaker. Spotless is the baseline; extra credit goes to shops that clearly take pride in every corner.
  • out of 10 Atmosphere & Soul Does the space have a personality? Does the lighting, music, and decor make you feel something — or does it feel like a beige waiting room with an espresso machine?
